Core vision

Agentic AI, learned and run by your own teams.

Coral Petal AI is an Agentic AI consultancy. We design, build and run AI agents inside enterprises and governments, end to end.

Our core vision goes one step further: to enable enterprise teams to make the best of Agentic AI, so they can learn it, deploy it and keep finding new use cases across the whole company.
