Services

Agentic AI, delivered end to end.

One partner from the first workshop to agents running in production, with security, controls and your people built in from day one.

01 · Strategy & readiness

Start with the problems worth solving.

We map your processes, data and risks, then rank Agentic AI use cases by value and feasibility, so you invest first where it pays back.

What you get

  • Use-case discovery and prioritization workshops
  • Data and platform readiness assessment
  • Reference architecture and build-vs-buy guidance
  • A costed, phased roadmap with clear owners

02 · Implementation

Agents that act inside your systems.

We design, build and integrate agents that reason, plan and use your tools: infrastructure, development, integration and deployment, end to end.

What we build

  • Single and multi-agent workflows with tool use
  • Retrieval over your documents and enterprise data
  • Integrations with ERP, CRM and line-of-business systems
  • Cloud deployment on Azure and other major platforms
  • Human-in-the-loop review where decisions matter

03 · Run & govern

Built for the day after launch.

Agents need evaluation, monitoring and guardrails to stay accurate, secure and compliant. We set these up and run them with you.

How we keep agents safe

  • Evaluation suites tested against your real data
  • Monitoring for quality, cost and drift
  • Policy, privacy and access guardrails
  • Clear handover so your team owns the system

04 · Team enablement

People who can build and use agents.

Through Coral Petal Academy and live corporate training, your teams learn the concepts and the enterprise implementation, so humans and agents work together.
