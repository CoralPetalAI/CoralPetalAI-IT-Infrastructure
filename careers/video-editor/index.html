Video Editor

Turn raw recordings into polished, enterprise-grade video for our courses, walkthroughs, client work and YouTube, with AI agents in your everyday workflow.

The role

What you'll do

Edit to a brand standard

Pacing, cuts, color, audio, captions, lower thirds and motion graphics.

Build learning content

Turn screen recordings, talking heads and slides into clear lessons.

Work with AI agents

Transcripts, rough cuts, B-roll, subtitles and translations, reviewed by you.

Keep a human sign-off

Brief agents, check their output and approve everything that ships.

Create reusable templates

Presets and checklists so every video meets the same bar.

Deliver everywhere

Versions and formats for web, LinkedIn, YouTube and learning platforms.

You

What you bring

  • A strong editing portfolio, ideally corporate, training or technical content
  • Expert in Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve or Final Cut Pro; After Effects for motion
  • Strong Figma: slides, thumbnails, title cards and on-screen graphics
  • Hands-on with AI video and audio tools, and with delegating to AI agents
  • A sharp eye for detail, accessibility and a consistent brand
  • Clear writing and comfort working remotely and asynchronously

Nice to have: Data science, AI or cloud topics YouTube optimization
